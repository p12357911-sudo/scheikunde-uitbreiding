26.q In the reaction 2 Mg + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 MgO, 0.30 mol of Mg reacts. How many moles of O<sub>2</sub> are needed or formed? Round to 2 significant figures.

26.e Mg : O<sub>2</sub> = 2 : 1, so 0.30 mol × 1 / 2 = 0.15 mol
