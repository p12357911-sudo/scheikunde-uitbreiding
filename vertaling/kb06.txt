### sk4h-atoombouw-KB
heading Atomic structure and the periodic table
0.q What charge do a proton, a neutron and an electron have?
0.a Proton positive (1+), neutron no charge, electron negative (1−)
1.q Where is almost all the mass of an atom?
1.a In the nucleus
2.q What is the atomic number?
2.a The number of protons
3.q What is the mass number?
3.a The number of protons plus the number of neutrons
4.q How do you calculate the number of neutrons?
4.a Mass number minus atomic number
5.q A chlorine atom has mass number 35 and atomic number 17. How many neutrons does it have?
5.a 35 − 17 = 18
6.q How many electrons does a neutral atom have?
6.a The same as the number of protons
7.q What are isotopes?
7.a Atoms of the same element with a different number of neutrons
8.q What is the unit of atomic mass?
8.a u
9.q Why is the atomic mass in BINAS table 99 not a whole number?
9.a It is an average of the masses of the isotopes
10.q What are the rows and the columns of the periodic table called?
10.a Rows: periods. Columns: groups.
11.q What do elements in the same group have in common?
11.a The same number of valence electrons
12.q What do elements in the same period have in common?
12.a The same number of shells containing electrons
13.q What is the maximum number of electrons in the K, L and M shells?
13.a K: 2, L: 8, M: 18
14.q What are valence electrons?
14.a The electrons in the outer shell
15.q What is the electron arrangement of oxygen (atomic number 8)?
15.a K = 2, L = 6
16.q What is the electron arrangement of chlorine (atomic number 17)?
16.a K = 2, L = 8, M = 7
17.q How many valence electrons does an element in group 17 have?
17.a 7
18.q What is a noble gas configuration?
18.a An outer shell that is completely filled
19.q Which group contains the noble gases?
19.a Group 18
20.q What are the elements in group 1 (except hydrogen) and in group 17 called?
20.a Group 1: alkali metals. Group 17: halogens.
21.q What is an ion?
21.a A charged particle (an atom that has gained or lost electrons)
22.q What happens when sodium (atomic number 11) becomes a Na<sup>+</sup> ion?
22.a It loses 1 electron
23.q How many electrons does an O<sup>2-</sup> ion have (atomic number 8)?
23.a 10
24.q Which ions do metals form and which ions do non-metals form?
24.a Metals form positive ions, non-metals form negative ions
25.q How many protons, neutrons and electrons does an atom of Na-23 have? (atomic number 11)
25.a Protons = atomic number = 11; neutrons = 23 − 11 = 12; electrons = 11 (the atom is neutral)
26.q How many protons, neutrons and electrons does the ion Mg<sup>2+</sup> with mass number 24 have? (atomic number 12)
26.a Protons = 12; neutrons = 24 − 12 = 12; electrons = 12 − 2 = 10
27.q What is the electron arrangement of aluminium? (atomic number 13)
27.a K = 2, L = 8, M = 3
28.q Which ion does a calcium atom form? (atomic number 20)
28.a Calcium is in group 2: the atom loses 2 electrons and becomes Ca<sup>2+</sup>
29.q How many electrons does the ion S<sup>2-</sup> have? (atomic number 16)
29.a 16 + 2 = 18 electrons
30.q An atom has the electron arrangement K = 2, L = 8, M = 5. In which group and period is the element?
30.a 3 shells means period 3; 5 valence electrons means group 15 (phosphorus)
31.q Boron occurs as B-10 (19.9%) and B-11 (80.1%). Calculate the average atomic mass, using the mass numbers as the masses.
31.a (10 × 19.9 + 11 × 80.1) : 100 = 10.8 u
32.q Which ion charge do elements from group 2 and from group 16 form?
32.a Group 2: 2+ (lose two electrons); group 16: 2- (gain two electrons)
33.q How do you calculate the average atomic mass of an element with several isotopes?
33.a Multiply the mass of each isotope by its percentage, add them up and divide by 100 (weighted average)
34.q How many shells and how many valence electrons does potassium have? (atomic number 19)
34.a 4 shells (K = 2, L = 8, M = 8, N = 1) and 1 valence electron
### sk4h-atoombouw-rekenen-KB
heading Chemical calculations
0.q Which three particles do you find in an atom? Also give their abbreviations
0.a Protons, neutrons and electrons; p, n, e<sup>-</sup>
1.q Which particles give the atom most of its mass?
1.a Protons and neutrons
2.q In which unit is the mass of atoms expressed?
2.a atomic mass units (u)
3.q What determines the atomic number?
3.a The number of protons in the nucleus
4.q What does the mass number show?
4.a mass number = the number of protons + the number of neutrons
5.q What is the overview of all atoms called?
5.a The periodic table
6.q What is the ratio between protons and electrons? Explain why.
6.a Protons have an elementary charge of 1+, electrons of -1. In the atomic model atoms are neutral. So there are as many electrons as protons.
7.q Name two atomic models and one difference between them.
7.a The Rutherford model, in which electrons move around a nucleus in 1 cloud, and the Bohr model, in which electrons move around the nucleus in shells.
8.q Where are the 3 atomic particles located?
8.a p and n in the nucleus, electrons in different shells relatively far from the nucleus
9.q What are the outermost electrons called?
9.a Valence electrons
10.q Name 3 electron shells, their position relative to each other and how many electrons they can hold
10.a Seen from the nucleus the first three shells are K, L, M; with 2, 8 and 18 electrons respectively.
11.q What is meant by noble gas configuration?
11.a The electron configuration in which the shells are exactly filled to the maximum.
12.q How many valence electrons does magnesium have? (use BINAS table 99)
12.a Magnesium has atomic number 12 (BINAS table 99), with configuration K = 2, L = 8 and M = 2, so there are 2 electrons in the outer shell, so 2 valence electrons.
13.q What does magnesium need to reach the noble gas configuration?
13.a Lose two electrons from the M shell; Mg<sup>2+</sup> is formed
14.q What is the molecular mass of sulfuric acid, H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>? (use BINAS table 99)
14.a 2 × 1.008 + 32.06 + 4 × 16.00 = 98.08 u (BINAS table 99)
15.q In which group are the halogens?
15.a in group 17
16.q In which group are the noble gases?
16.a in group 18
17.q Elements in a group have similar …
17.a chemical properties
18.q How can sodium reach a noble gas configuration?
18.a By losing its one valence electron; Na<sup>+</sup> is formed.
19.q How can fluorine (F) reach a noble gas configuration?
19.a F has atomic number 9: with K: 2 and L: 7 electrons. By gaining an electron F gets the noble gas configuration and F<sup>-</sup> is formed
20.q Explain what isotopes are.
20.a Isotopes are atoms with the same atomic number but a different mass number. They have the same number of protons and a different number of neutrons.
21.q The mass number in the periodic table is almost never a round number. Explain what isotopes have to do with this.
21.a The mass number in the periodic table is the weighted average of the different isotopes that occur in nature.
22.q The relative atomic mass of chlorine is 35.45 u and the natural isotopes are Cl-35 and Cl-37. Explain which isotope is the most abundant in nature.
22.a 35.45 u is closer to 35 u than to 37 u. So Cl-35 will occur more often than Cl-37. Check: BINAS table 25A: 75.78% is Cl-35 and 24.22% is Cl-37.
23.q BINAS table 25A: which isotopes of bromine occur in nature?
23.a Br-79 and Br-81
24.q Give the atomic composition of both isotopes of bromine.
24.a Br-79: Br has atomic number 35, so 35 protons. The number of neutrons is 79 − 35 = 44. There are 35 electrons arranged over four shells: 2, 8, 18, 7. Br-81: also has 35 protons, but (81 − 35 =) 46 neutrons, and also 35 electrons arranged over four shells: 2, 8, 18, 7.
25.q How many hydrogen atoms are there in 3.5 mol of water?
25.a 1 mol of water contains 2 mol of H atoms, so 3.5 mol of water contains 2 × 3.5 = 7.0 mol of H atoms
26.q C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub> (g) and oxygen gas react to form carbon dioxide and water. Write the reaction equation using the step-by-step method.
26.a Step 1 (reaction scheme in words): ethane (g) + oxygen (g) -> carbon dioxide (g) + water (l). Step 2 (reaction scheme in molecular formulae): C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub> (g) + O<sub>2</sub> (g) -> CO<sub>2</sub> (g) + H<sub>2</sub>O (l). Step 3 (atom balance - balancing): C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub> (g) + 3½O<sub>2</sub> (g) -> 2CO<sub>2</sub> (g) + 3H<sub>2</sub>O (l). Step 4 (simplifying the reaction equation): 2C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub> (g) + 7O<sub>2</sub> (g) -> 4CO<sub>2</sub> (g) + 6H<sub>2</sub>O (l).
27.q In which mole ratio does ethane burn in the reaction above?
27.a 2 : 7 : 4 : 6 mol
28.q In a reactor you let 2 mol of ethane react with 14 mol of oxygen. Is there an excess or a shortage of oxygen?
28.a Excess (2× as much as needed)
29.q Give the scientific notation of 0.0023 mol
29.a 2.3 ∙ 10<sup>-3</sup> mol
30.q 1 L is … m<sup>3</sup>
30.a 0.001 m<sup>3</sup>
