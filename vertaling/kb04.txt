### nk3h-elektriciteit-KB
heading Electricity
0.q What does a generator do?
0.a It converts kinetic energy into electrical energy
1.q What is a power station?
1.a A place where electrical energy is generated
2.q Why is high voltage used to transport electricity over long distances?
2.a Less energy is then lost as heat in the cables
3.q What does a transformer do?
3.a It makes the voltage higher or lower
4.q What is the unit of voltage?
4.a Volt (V)
5.q What is the unit of power?
5.a Watt (W)
6.q Which unit is on your energy bill?
6.a Kilowatt-hour (kWh)
7.q Give the formula for power
7.a P = E : t (power = energy : time)
8.q Give the formula for energy
8.a E = P × t (energy = power × time)
9.q Which units belong to P, E and t in the formula P = E : t?
9.a P in watts (W), E in joules (J), t in seconds (s)
10.q What is a kilowatt-hour?
10.a The energy used by a 1 kW appliance in 1 hour
11.q How many kW is 1500 W?
11.a 1.5 kW
12.q A 2.0 kW appliance is switched on for 3.0 hours. How many kWh does it use?
12.a 2.0 kW × 3.0 h = 6.0 kWh
13.q One kilowatt-hour costs € 0.30. What do 5 kWh cost?
13.a 5 × € 0.30 = € 1.50
14.q An appliance uses 800 J in 20 s. What is its power?
14.a 800 J : 20 s = 40 W
15.q A 60 W lamp is on for 10 s. How much energy does the lamp use?
15.a 60 W × 10 s = 600 J
16.q What is the voltage of a household socket?
16.a 230 V
17.q Which instrument measures voltage and current?
17.a A multimeter
18.q Which wire is at a high voltage (live)?
18.a The live wire
19.q What does a fuse do?
19.a It breaks the circuit when the current is too high
20.q What is overload?
20.a Too many appliances are connected at the same time, so the current becomes too high
21.q What is a short circuit?
21.a The current flows along a path with almost no resistance
22.q What does a residual-current device (RCD) do?
22.a It switches off the current if current leaks away to earth
23.q What is the earth wire for?
23.a For safety if an appliance develops a fault
24.q Why must you not touch a socket with wet hands?
24.a Water conducts electric current, so you could get an electric shock
25.q What is the unit of current?
25.a Ampere (A)
26.q Which formula applies to electrical power?
26.a P = U × I
27.q A washing machine works on 230 V and draws 10 A. What is its power?
27.a P = U × I = 230 × 10 = 2300 W
28.q A 1840 W hairdryer is connected to 230 V. What is the current?
28.a I = P / U = 1840 / 230 = 8.0 A
29.q How many joules is 1 kWh?
29.a 1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3.6 × 10<sup>6</sup> J
30.q A 1200 W appliance is on for 2.5 hours. One kWh costs € 0.30. How much does that cost?
30.a E = 1.2 kW × 2.5 h = 3.0 kWh; cost = 3.0 × 0.30 = € 0.90
31.q Convert 250 mA to A.
31.a 250 mA = 250 / 1000 = 0.250 A
32.q A 4.0 W appliance is on standby day and night for a whole year (365 days). How many kWh is that?
32.a 4.0 W × 24 h × 365 = 35 040 Wh ≈ 35 kWh
33.q You have lamps of 11 W, 40 W and 60 W. Which one uses the most energy in 1 hour?
33.a 60 W uses 60 Wh, the other lamps use less
34.q What is the device called with which an energy company measures your consumption?
34.a A kWh meter (energy meter)
### nk3h-energie-KB
heading Energy
0.q Which type of energy does a moving bicycle have?
0.a Kinetic energy
1.q In which type of energy is the energy stored in petrol?
1.a Chemical energy
2.q Which of these energy sources is a fossil fuel?
2.a Natural gas
3.q Which of these energy sources is renewable (sustainable)?
3.a Wind energy
4.q Which energy conversion takes place in a solar cell?
4.a Radiant energy becomes electrical energy
5.q Which energy conversion takes place in a bicycle dynamo?
5.a Kinetic energy becomes electrical energy
6.q Which energy conversion takes place in an electric motor?
6.a Electrical energy becomes kinetic energy
7.q Name two renewable energy sources.
7.a For example solar energy, wind energy, hydroelectric power, biomass or geothermal energy
8.q What does the law of conservation of energy state?
8.a Energy is not lost but is converted into another form
9.q Which formula goes with heating a substance?
9.a Q = m × c × ΔT
10.q You heat 2.0 kg of water from 15 °C to 65 °C. The specific heat capacity of water is 4.18 × 10<sup>3</sup> J/(kg·K). How much energy is needed?
10.a Q = m × c × ΔT = 2.0 × 4180 × 50 = 4.2 × 10<sup>5</sup> J
11.q You heat 0.50 kg of iron by 20 K. The specific heat capacity of iron is 4.5 × 10<sup>2</sup> J/(kg·K). How much energy is needed?
11.a Q = 0.50 × 450 × 20 = 4.5 × 10<sup>3</sup> J
12.q Heating 0.50 kg of a substance by 6.0 K takes 12 600 J. Calculate the specific heat capacity.
12.a c = Q / (m × ΔT) = 12 600 / (0.50 × 6.0) = 4.2 × 10<sup>3</sup> J/(kg·K)
13.q 84 kJ of heat is added to 2.0 kg of water (c = 4.2 × 10<sup>3</sup> J/(kg·K)). By how many K does the temperature rise?
13.a ΔT = Q / (m × c) = 84 000 / (2.0 × 4200) = 10 K
14.q A 2000 W kettle heats 1.5 kg of water from 20 °C to 100 °C (c = 4.18 × 10<sup>3</sup> J/(kg·K)). No energy is lost. How long does it take?
14.a Q = 1.5 × 4180 × 80 = 5.0 × 10<sup>5</sup> J; t = E / P = 5.0 × 10<sup>5</sup> / 2000 = 2.5 × 10<sup>2</sup> s
15.q What is the efficiency of an appliance?
15.a Useful energy divided by total energy, times 100%
16.q An appliance converts 1200 J. Of this, 360 J is useful. What is the efficiency?
16.a efficiency = 360 / 1200 × 100% = 30%
17.q A 60 W incandescent lamp gives off 3.0 W as light. What is the efficiency?
17.a efficiency = 3.0 / 60 × 100% = 5.0%
18.q An appliance has an efficiency of 40%. The useful energy is 200 J. How much energy went in altogether?
18.a total = 200 / 0.40 = 500 J
19.q An appliance with an efficiency of 25% receives 800 J. How much of it is useful?
19.a useful = 0.25 × 800 = 200 J
20.q A central heating boiler receives 64 MJ of energy from natural gas. The efficiency is 90%. How much energy is used usefully?
20.a 0.90 × 64 = 57.6, so 58 MJ
21.q A 1.5 kW heater is on for 2.0 hours. That is 3.0 kWh. How many MJ is that? (1 kWh = 3.6 MJ)
21.a 3.0 × 3.6 = 10.8, so 11 MJ
22.q In which way is heat transported through a metal pan?
22.a By conduction
23.q In which way does heat reach the Earth from the Sun?
23.a By radiation
24.q In which way does warm air spread through a room?
24.a By convection
25.q Which material is a good thermal insulator?
25.a Polystyrene foam
26.q Why does double glazing insulate better than single glazing?
26.a The gas between the panes conducts heat poorly
27.q Why are the handles of pans often made of plastic?
27.a Plastic conducts heat poorly
28.q What is the unit of specific heat capacity?
28.a J/(kg·K)
29.q What happens to energy that seems to be "lost" through friction?
29.a It is converted into heat
30.q Which substance is used as fuel in a nuclear power station?
30.a Uranium
31.q For gravitational potential energy: E<sub>z</sub> = m × g × h. Here g = 9.8 N/kg. Calculate the gravitational potential energy of 2.0 kg at a height of 5.0 m.
31.a E<sub>z</sub> = 2.0 × 9.8 × 5.0 = 98 J
32.q For kinetic energy: E<sub>k</sub> = ½ × m × v<sup>2</sup>. Calculate the kinetic energy of a 1000 kg car travelling at 10 m/s.
32.a E<sub>k</sub> = ½ × 1000 × 10<sup>2</sup> = 5.0 × 10<sup>4</sup> J
33.q Name the three ways in which heat is transported.
33.a Conduction, convection and radiation
34.q An 8.0 W LED lamp and a 40 W incandescent lamp give the same amount of light. How many times as much energy does the incandescent lamp use in the same time?
34.a 40 / 8.0 = 5.0 times
### nk3h-kracht-en-beweging-KB
heading Force and motion
0.q What is the SI unit of speed?
0.a m/s
1.q Convert 20 m/s to km/h.
1.a 20 × 3.6 = 72 km/h
2.q Convert 54 km/h to m/s.
2.a 54 / 3.6 = 15 m/s
3.q Which formula applies to speed when the speed is constant?
3.a v = s / t
4.q A cyclist travels at 5.0 m/s. How far does he get in 12 s?
4.a s = v × t = 5.0 × 12 = 60 m
5.q A runner runs 150 m at a speed of 6.0 m/s. How long does it take?
5.a t = s / v = 150 / 6.0 = 25 s
6.q A walker covers 3.0 km in 20 minutes. What is his average speed in km/h?
6.a 20 min = 1/3 h; v = 3.0 / (1/3) = 9.0 km/h
7.q A car travels 60 km in 1.0 hour and then 100 km in 1.0 hour. What is the average speed?
7.a (60 + 100) km / 2.0 h = 80 km/h
8.q What do you see in a (s,t) graph with a horizontal line?
8.a The object is at rest
9.q What does a straight, sloping upward line in a (s,t) graph mean?
9.a Constant speed
10.q What does a horizontal line in a (v,t) graph mean?
10.a Constant speed
11.q What does a straight, sloping upward line in a (v,t) graph mean?
11.a Constant acceleration
12.q How do you find the distance travelled from a (v,t) graph?
12.a From the area under the graph
13.q In a (v,t) graph the speed is constant at 10 m/s for 6.0 s. How far does the object travel?
13.a area = 10 × 6.0 = 60 m
14.q What is the SI unit of acceleration?
14.a m/s<sup>2</sup>
15.q A car accelerates from 0 to 20 m/s in 8.0 s. What is the average acceleration?
15.a a = Δv / t = 20 / 8.0 = 2.5 m/s<sup>2</sup>
16.q A car brakes from 25 m/s to a standstill in 5.0 s. What is the deceleration?
16.a a = Δv / t = 25 / 5.0 = 5.0 m/s<sup>2</sup>
17.q A 1200 kg car accelerates at 2.0 m/s<sup>2</sup>. What is the resultant force?
17.a F = m × a = 1200 × 2.0 = 2.4 × 10<sup>3</sup> N
18.q A resultant force of 300 N gives an object an acceleration of 1.5 m/s<sup>2</sup>. What is the mass?
18.a m = F / a = 300 / 1.5 = 200 kg
19.q A resultant force of 50 N acts on a 10 kg cart. What is the acceleration?
19.a a = F / m = 50 / 10 = 5.0 m/s<sup>2</sup>
20.q What happens to an object when the resultant force is 0 N?
20.a It stays at rest or moves at constant speed
21.q The driving force of a car is 500 N and the friction force is also 500 N. What kind of motion does the car make?
21.a Motion at constant speed
22.q The driving force is 800 N and the friction force is 300 N. What is the resultant force?
22.a F<sub>res</sub> = 800 − 300 = 500 N in the direction of travel; the car accelerates
23.q What does Newton's third law (action and reaction) state?
23.a Two objects exert forces of equal size and opposite direction on each other
24.q You push a crate 5.0 m forward with a force of 40 N. How much work do you do? (W = F × s)
24.a W = F × s = 40 × 5.0 = 200 J
25.q Lifting an object over 6.0 m takes 300 J of work. What is the force?
25.a F = W / s = 300 / 6.0 = 50 N
26.q A driver travels at 20 m/s and has a reaction time of 0.75 s. What is the reaction distance?
26.a s = v × t = 20 × 0.75 = 15 m
27.q The reaction distance is 14 m and the braking distance is 26 m. What is the stopping distance?
27.a stopping distance = reaction distance + braking distance = 14 + 26 = 40 m
28.q A car travels twice as fast. What happens to the braking distance?
28.a The braking distance becomes about 4 times as large
29.q A stone falls without air resistance. The acceleration of free fall is 9.8 m/s<sup>2</sup>. What is the speed after 2.0 s?
29.a v = g × t = 9.8 × 2.0 = 19.6 ≈ 20 m/s
30.q A parachutist descends at constant speed. Which statement is correct?
30.a The force of gravity is equal to the air resistance
31.q Why do you slide forward when a car suddenly brakes?
31.a Because of inertia you keep moving at the same speed
32.q Why does a crumple zone reduce the force on the occupants in a collision?
32.a The car slows down over a longer time, so the deceleration is smaller
33.q Which two parts make up the stopping distance?
33.a Reaction distance + braking distance
34.q How do you find the speed from a (s,t) graph?
34.a From the slope of the line: v = Δs / Δt
### nk3h-krachten-KB
heading Forces
0.q What is the unit of force?
0.a Newton (N)
1.q What do you use to measure a force?
1.a A newton meter (force meter)
2.q Name three types of force
2.a Gravity, friction, spring force, muscle force, magnetic force
3.q What can a force do to an object?
3.a Change its speed, its direction or its shape
4.q What is the force called with which the Earth pulls on an object?
4.a Gravity (force of gravity)
5.q Give the formula for the force of gravity
5.a Fz = m × g
6.q What is the value of g on Earth?
6.a 9.8 N/kg
7.q An object has a mass of 10 kg. What is the force of gravity on Earth?
7.a 10 kg × 9.8 N/kg = 98 N
8.q Which unit goes with mass?
8.a Kilogram (kg)
9.q You go to the Moon. Does your mass change?
9.a No. The force of gravity on you does become smaller.
10.q What is friction force?
10.a A force that opposes a motion
11.q What is spring force?
11.a The force with which a stretched or compressed spring pushes or pulls back
12.q What does the spring constant tell you?
12.a How stiff a spring is (how much force you need for a certain extension)
13.q A spring stretches 2 cm under 4 N. How many cm does it stretch under 8 N?
13.a 4 cm
14.q What is the resultant force?
14.a The single force that has the same effect as all the forces together
15.q A force of 30 N acts to the right and 20 N to the left. What is the resultant force?
15.a 10 N to the right
16.q How large is the resultant force when the forces are balanced?
16.a 0 N
17.q How do you draw a force?
17.a As an arrow: the length shows the size, the arrow points in the direction and starts at the point of application
18.q What is a lever?
18.a A bar that can turn about a pivot
19.q What is the pivot?
19.a The point about which a lever turns
20.q What is the lever arm of a force?
20.a The distance from the pivot to the force
21.q Which formula applies when a lever is balanced?
21.a Force × arm on the left = force × arm on the right
22.q A lever is balanced. On the left, 200 N acts at 1.0 m from the pivot. On the right, 100 N acts. At what distance?
22.a 200 N × 1.0 m = 100 N × r, so r = 2.0 m
23.q Why do you need less force to loosen a nut with a long spanner?
23.a A long spanner has a long arm
24.q Name two objects that are levers
24.a Crowbar, scissors, pliers, seesaw, wheelbarrow
25.q What is the counterweight of a tower crane for?
25.a To keep the crane balanced
26.q Calculate the force of gravity on a child of 40 kg. Use g = 9.8 N/kg.
26.a F<sub>z</sub> = m × g = 40 × 9.8 = 392 N
27.q A force of gravity of 29.4 N acts on an object. Use g = 9.8 N/kg. What is the mass?
27.a m = F<sub>z</sub> / g = 29.4 / 9.8 = 3.0 kg
28.q On the Moon g = 1.6 N/kg. What is the force of gravity on an object of 10 kg?
28.a F<sub>z</sub> = 10 × 1.6 = 16 N
29.q A spring becomes 3.0 cm longer under a force of 6.0 N. How much longer does the spring become under 10 N? (the spring stays within its elastic range)
29.a Extension is proportional to force: 3.0 × 10 / 6.0 = 5.0 cm
30.q A lever is balanced. On the left, 50 N acts at 0.40 m from the pivot. On the right the arm is 0.25 m. What is the force on the right?
30.a F<sub>1</sub> × r<sub>1</sub> = F<sub>2</sub> × r<sub>2</sub>; 50 × 0.40 = F × 0.25; F = 80 N
31.q What is the moment of a force of 20 N on an arm of 0.30 m?
31.a M = F × r = 20 × 0.30 = 6.0 N·m
32.q What does a fixed pulley do?
32.a It changes the direction of the force; the force stays the same size
33.q You lift a load of 200 N with a movable pulley (no friction, ropes vertical). How large a force must you exert?
33.a A movable pulley halves the force: 100 N (but you pull twice as much rope)
34.q A crate of 20 kg is at rest on the ground. Use g = 9.8 N/kg. How large is the force of the ground on the crate?
34.a The crate is at rest: the force of the ground is equal to the force of gravity: 20 × 9.8 = 196 N
