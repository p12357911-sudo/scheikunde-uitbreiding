### sk3h-brandstoffen-kunststoffen-KB
heading Fuels and plastics
0.q Which three substances are fossil fuels?
0.a Crude oil, natural gas and coal
1.q Why are crude oil, natural gas and coal called non-renewable fuels?
1.a They formed over millions of years and are running out
2.q Which fuel is renewable?
2.a Biodiesel
3.q How do you separate crude oil into different fractions?
3.a By distillation, based on the difference in boiling point
4.q Which fraction of crude oil has the lowest boiling point?
4.a Gas (light substances)
5.q Which fraction of crude oil has the highest boiling point and stays at the bottom of the distillation tower?
5.a Bitumen
6.q What does a hydrocarbon consist of?
6.a Only carbon and hydrogen atoms
7.q What is the molecular formula of methane?
7.a CH<sub>4</sub>
8.q What is the molecular formula of propane?
8.a C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub>
9.q What is the molecular formula of butane?
9.a C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>10</sub>
10.q How many hydrogen atoms does an alkane with 5 carbon atoms have? An alkane has the formula CnH<sub>2</sub>n+2.
10.a 12
11.q Which name belongs to C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub>?
11.a Ethane
12.q Which substances are formed in the complete combustion of a hydrocarbon?
12.a Carbon dioxide and water
13.q Which substances can be formed in the incomplete combustion of a hydrocarbon?
13.a Carbon monoxide, soot and water
14.q Why is incomplete combustion dangerous?
14.a Poisonous carbon monoxide is formed
15.q How does incomplete combustion happen?
15.a There is too little oxygen
16.q Which equation shows the complete combustion of methane?
16.a CH<sub>4</sub> + 2 O<sub>2</sub> → CO<sub>2</sub> + 2 H<sub>2</sub>O
17.q Which number must go in front of O<sub>2</sub>? C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub> + … O<sub>2</sub> → 3 CO<sub>2</sub> + 4 H<sub>2</sub>O
17.a 5
18.q Which equation shows the complete combustion of butane?
18.a 2 C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>10</sub> + 13 O<sub>2</sub> → 8 CO<sub>2</sub> + 10 H<sub>2</sub>O
19.q Which gas is mainly responsible for the enhanced greenhouse effect?
19.a Carbon dioxide
20.q Which two substances cause acid rain?
20.a Sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides
21.q What are the three conditions for combustion (the fire triangle)?
21.a Fuel, oxygen and a high enough temperature
22.q You put out a burning pan with a lid. Which condition do you remove?
22.a Oxygen
23.q Is burning an exothermic or an endothermic reaction?
23.a Exothermic: heat is released
24.q What is formed when hydrogen burns?
24.a Water
25.q Which equation shows the combustion of hydrogen?
25.a 2 H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 H<sub>2</sub>O
26.q What is a polymer?
26.a A substance with very long chain molecules
27.q What is a monomer?
27.a The small molecule from which a polymer is built up
28.q From which monomer is polyethene made?
28.a Ethene
29.q What is a thermoplastic?
29.a A plastic that softens when heated and can then be reshaped
30.q What is a thermosetting plastic?
30.a A plastic that cannot be reshaped once it has hardened
31.q Which plastic is an elastomer?
31.a Rubber
32.q Why is it bad for the environment to throw plastic away in nature?
32.a It hardly breaks down and lasts for a very long time
33.q What is it called when plastic is reused to make new products?
33.a Recycling
34.q Name two disadvantages of burning fossil fuels
34.a Carbon dioxide is released (greenhouse effect) and the fuels are running out. Sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides also cause acid rain
### sk3h-reacties-KB
heading Chemical reactions 2
0.q What are starting substances (reactants)?
0.a The substances that react with each other (left of the arrow)
1.q What are reaction products?
1.a The substances that are formed (right of the arrow)
2.q Name three observations that can indicate a chemical reaction
2.a Colour change, gas being produced, precipitate, change of smell, temperature change, light
3.q Is melting ice a chemical reaction? Explain.
3.a No. No new substance is formed (only a different state).
4.q Which two things stay the same in a chemical reaction?
4.a The number of atoms of each type and the total mass
5.q What is step 1 of the step-by-step method for a reaction equation?
5.a Write down the reaction scheme in words
6.q What is step 2 of the step-by-step method for a reaction equation?
6.a Write down the reaction scheme in molecular formulae
7.q What is step 3 of the step-by-step method for a reaction equation?
7.a Balance the atoms
8.q What is step 4 of the step-by-step method for a reaction equation?
8.a Simplify the reaction equation
9.q What may you change when balancing a reaction equation?
9.a Only the numbers in front of the formulae (coefficients). Not the small numbers inside the formulae.
10.q Balance: H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub> → H<sub>2</sub>O
10.a 2 H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 H<sub>2</sub>O
11.q Balance: Mg + O<sub>2</sub> → MgO
11.a 2 Mg + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 MgO
12.q Balance: N<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub> → NH<sub>3</sub>
12.a N<sub>2</sub> + 3 H<sub>2</sub> → 2 NH<sub>3</sub>
13.q Balance: Fe + O<sub>2</sub> → Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
13.a 4 Fe + 3 O<sub>2</sub> → 2 Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
14.q Give the reaction equation for the complete combustion of methane
14.a CH<sub>4</sub> + 2 O<sub>2</sub> → CO<sub>2</sub> + 2 H<sub>2</sub>O
15.q Simplify: 4 H<sub>2</sub> + 2 O<sub>2</sub> → 4 H<sub>2</sub>O
15.a 2 H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 H<sub>2</sub>O
16.q What do (s), (l), (g) and (aq) after a formula mean?
16.a (s) solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas, (aq) dissolved in water
17.q What is decomposition?
17.a Splitting a substance into two or more other substances
18.q What is a synthesis?
18.a Making one new substance from two or more substances
19.q What is it called when a substance is decomposed using electric current?
19.a Electrolysis
20.q Give the reaction equation for the decomposition of water
20.a 2 H<sub>2</sub>O → 2 H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub>
21.q What is combustion?
21.a A reaction of a substance with oxygen
22.q Which three things do you need for combustion?
22.a Fuel, oxygen and a high enough temperature
23.q Which substances are formed in the complete combustion of a hydrocarbon?
23.a Carbon dioxide and water
24.q Which substances can be formed in incomplete combustion?
24.a Carbon monoxide and soot (carbon)
25.q What is a precipitate?
25.a A solid that forms in a solution
26.q Balance: Zn + HCl → ZnCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>
26.a Zn + 2 HCl → ZnCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>
27.q Balance: H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> → H<sub>2</sub>O + O<sub>2</sub>
27.a 2 H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> → 2 H<sub>2</sub>O + O<sub>2</sub>
28.q Balance: Ca + H<sub>2</sub>O → Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>
28.a Ca + 2 H<sub>2</sub>O → Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>
29.q Balance: Al + O<sub>2</sub> → Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
29.a 4 Al + 3 O<sub>2</sub> → 2 Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
30.q You mix a solution of silver nitrate with a solution of sodium chloride. Silver chloride does not dissolve in water. What do you see?
30.a A white precipitate of silver chloride
31.q How do you test for oxygen?
31.a A glowing splint burns more brightly
32.q How do you test for carbon dioxide?
32.a With limewater: it turns cloudy
33.q How do you test for hydrogen gas?
33.a With a burning splint: you hear a squeaky pop
34.q Which reaction equation of sodium with chlorine is balanced?
34.a 2 Na + Cl<sub>2</sub> → 2 NaCl

heading Calculations on reactions
intro.1 If the question does not say how to round, round to one decimal place.
0.q What does the law of conservation of mass state?
0.a The total mass before the reaction is equal to the total mass after the reaction
1.q In a reaction in a closed bottle, 12 g of magnesium reacts with 8 g of oxygen. How many grams of magnesium oxide are formed?
1.a 20 g
2.q When 10.0 g of limestone (CaCO<sub>3</sub>) is heated, 5.6 g of calcium oxide and carbon dioxide are formed. How many grams of carbon dioxide are formed?
2.a 4.4 g
3.q You burn steel wool in the open air. The mass increases. Why is that?
3.a The steel wool takes up oxygen from the air
4.q A candle burns in the open air and the mass decreases. Why is that?
4.a The gases formed, carbon dioxide and water vapour, escape into the air
5.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of H<sub>2</sub>O. Atomic masses: H = 1.0 u, O = 16.0 u.
5.a 18 u
6.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of CO<sub>2</sub>. Atomic masses: C = 12.0 u, O = 16.0 u.
6.a 44 u
7.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of NH<sub>3</sub>. Atomic masses: N = 14.0 u, H = 1.0 u.
7.a 17 u
8.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>. Atomic masses: H = 1.0 u, S = 32.0 u, O = 16.0 u.
8.a 98 u
9.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of CaCO<sub>3</sub>. Atomic masses: Ca = 40.0 u, C = 12.0 u, O = 16.0 u.
9.a 100 u
10.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub>. Atomic masses: Ca = 40.0 u, O = 16.0 u, H = 1.0 u.
10.a 74 u
11.q Calculate the molecular mass (or formula mass) of Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>. Atomic masses: Fe = 56.0 u, O = 16.0 u.

14.q Calculate the mass percentage of iron in Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>. Atomic masses: Fe = 56.0 u, O = 16.0 u.
14.a 70.0%

15.a 36.0 g

16.a 44.0 g

17.a 28.0 g
18.q Magnesium burns: 2 Mg + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 MgO. Atomic masses: Mg = 24.0 u, O = 16.0 u. How many grams of magnesium oxide are formed from 12.0 g of magnesium?
18.a 20.0 g
19.q Magnesium and oxygen react in the mass ratio 3 : 2. How many grams of oxygen are needed for 9.0 g of magnesium?
19.a 6.0 g
20.q Copper and sulfur react to form copper(I) sulfide in the mass ratio 4 : 1. How many grams of copper(I) sulfide are formed from 8.0 g of copper and 2.0 g of sulfur?
20.a 10.0 g
21.q Iron and sulfur react in the mass ratio 7 : 4. How many grams of sulfur do you need for 14 g of iron?
21.a 8 g
22.q The molar mass of water is 18 g/mol. How many moles of water are in 36 g of water?
22.a 2.0 mol

26.q In the reaction 2 H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 H<sub>2</sub>O, 3.0 mol of H<sub>2</sub> reacts. How many moles of H<sub>2</sub>O are formed?
26.a 3.0 mol

27.a 2.0 mol
28.q In the reaction N<sub>2</sub> + 3 H<sub>2</sub> → 2 NH<sub>3</sub>, 6.0 mol of H<sub>2</sub> reacts. How many moles of NH<sub>3</sub> are formed?

29.a 0.50 mol

31.q What is the mass of 2.0 mol of CO<sub>2</sub>? Molar mass of CO<sub>2</sub> = 44 g/mol.
31.a 88 g
32.q What can you read from 2 H<sub>2</sub> + O<sub>2</sub> → 2 H<sub>2</sub>O?
32.a 2 mol of H<sub>2</sub> reacts with 1 mol of O<sub>2</sub> to form 2 mol of H<sub>2</sub>O

33.a 0.5 mol

34.a 44 g/mol
