### sk3h-atomen-moleculen-KB
heading Atoms and molecules
0.q Describe a chemical reaction
0.a In a chemical reaction the starting substances disappear and new substances with new properties are formed
1.q What are the smallest particles that determine the properties of a substance?
1.a Molecules
2.q What do molecules consist of?
2.a One or more types of atoms
3.q Atoms are also called …
3.a Elements
4.q Give the names of: Al, B, Ca, Cl, P, Fe, I, K, C, Cu
4.a Aluminium, Boron, Calcium, Chlorine, Phosphorus, Iron, Iodine, Potassium, Carbon, Copper
5.q Give the atomic symbol of: Bromine, Mercury, Lead, Magnesium, Sodium, Nitrogen, Uranium, Hydrogen, Silver, Zinc, Oxygen, Sulfur
5.a Br, Hg, Pb, Mg, Na, N, U, H, Ag, Zn, O, S
6.q What does the molecular formula show?
6.a Which atoms, and how many of each, a molecule consists of
7.q In H<sub>2</sub>O the 2 is the subscript. What does the 2 show?
7.a That there are 2 hydrogen atoms (and 1 oxygen atom) in H<sub>2</sub>O, water
8.q Give the molecular formula of: ammonia, carbon dioxide, methane, hydrogen peroxide, ethanol, water
8.a NH<sub>3</sub>, CO<sub>2</sub>, CH<sub>4</sub>, H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub>, C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>6</sub>O, H<sub>2</sub>O
9.q Elemental substances consist of 1 type of atom. Name 3.
9.a Br<sub>2</sub>, Cl<sub>2</sub>, F<sub>2</sub>, N<sub>2</sub>, O<sub>2</sub>, H<sub>2</sub>
10.q What is H<sub>2</sub> (g)?
10.a Hydrogen gas
11.q What is the ending of the name for the second type of atom in: chlorine, oxygen, sulfur, fluorine?
11.a chloride, oxide, sulfide, fluoride
12.q Give the Greek numerical prefixes for 1 to 8
12.a mono, di, tri, tetra, penta, hexa, hepta, octa
13.q Give the molecular formula of dinitrogen tetroxide
13.a N<sub>2</sub>O<sub>4</sub>
14.q Give the systematic name of P<sub>2</sub>S<sub>5</sub>
14.a diphosphorus pentasulfide
15.q Describe the term trivial name
15.a The everyday name of a substance
16.q Describe systematic name
16.a A name of a substance that is built up according to systematic rules
17.q How many atoms and how many types of atoms are in C<sub>8</sub>H<sub>10</sub>N<sub>4</sub>O<sub>2</sub>?
17.a 24 atoms; 4 types of atoms
18.q Which atomic symbol belongs to sodium?
18.a Na
19.q Which atomic symbol belongs to potassium?
19.a K
20.q Which element has the symbol Fe?
20.a Iron
21.q Which element has the symbol Pb?
21.a Lead
22.q Which element has the symbol Hg?
22.a Mercury
23.q Which element has the symbol Cu?
23.a Copper
24.q How many atoms are in one molecule of H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>?
24.a 7
25.q How many atoms are there in total in 3 H<sub>2</sub>O?
25.a 9
26.q How many hydrogen atoms are there in total in 2 CH<sub>4</sub>?
26.a 8
27.q What is the molecular formula of carbon monoxide?
27.a CO
28.q What is the systematic name of CO<sub>2</sub>?
28.a Carbon dioxide
29.q What is the systematic name of SO<sub>3</sub>?
29.a Sulfur trioxide
30.q Write steps 1 and 2 of the step-by-step method for the formation of ammonia from nitrogen and hydrogen
30.a Step 1: nitrogen + hydrogen → ammonia. Step 2: N<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub> → NH<sub>3</sub>
31.q How many atoms and how many types of atoms are in Ca(OH)<sub>2</sub>?
31.a 5 atoms; 3 types of atoms
32.q Balance: Na + Cl<sub>2</sub> → NaCl
32.a 2 Na + Cl<sub>2</sub> → 2 NaCl
33.q Balance: Al + O<sub>2</sub> → Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
33.a 4 Al + 3 O<sub>2</sub> → 2 Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
34.q Give the systematic name and the formula of the substance that consists of 1 carbon atom and 2 oxygen atoms
34.a Carbon dioxide, CO<sub>2</sub>
### sk3h-bouw-van-stoffen-KB
heading Structure of substances
0.q Which particles are in the nucleus of an atom?
0.a Protons and neutrons
1.q Which particle in an atom has no charge?
1.a Neutron
2.q What does the atomic number show?
2.a The number of protons in the nucleus
3.q What does the mass number show?
3.a The number of protons plus neutrons
4.q An atom has 11 protons and 12 neutrons. What is the mass number?
4.a 23
5.q An atom has atomic number 17 and mass number 35. How many neutrons does it have?
5.a 18
6.q How many electrons does a neutral atom with 15 protons have?
6.a 15
7.q What is the maximum number of electrons in the second electron shell?
7.a 8
8.q A sodium atom has 11 electrons. What is the electron arrangement across the shells?
8.a 2-8-1
9.q A chlorine atom has 17 electrons. What is the electron arrangement across the shells?
9.a 2-8-7
10.q An oxygen atom has atomic number 8. How many electrons are in the outer shell?
10.a 6
11.q Why do noble gases hardly react with other substances?
11.a Their outer shell is full
12.q What is an ion?
12.a A charged atom or group of atoms
13.q A sodium atom gives away one electron. Which ion is formed?
13.a Na<sup>+</sup>
14.q A chlorine atom takes up one electron. Which ion is formed?
14.a Cl<sup>−</sup>
15.q A magnesium atom (atomic number 12) wants to give away two electrons. Which ion is formed?
15.a Mg<sup>2+</sup>
16.q Which atoms usually form positive ions?
16.a Metal atoms
17.q How many electrons does an S<sup>2−</sup> ion have? Sulfur has atomic number 16.
17.a 18
18.q How many electrons does an Al<sup>3+</sup> ion have? Aluminium has atomic number 13.
18.a 10
19.q What is the formula of the salt that consists of Mg<sup>2+</sup> and Cl<sup>−</sup> ions?
19.a MgCl<sub>2</sub>
20.q What is the formula of the salt that consists of Ca<sup>2+</sup> and O<sup>2−</sup> ions?
20.a CaO
21.q What is the formula of the salt that consists of Al<sup>3+</sup> and O<sup>2−</sup> ions?
21.a Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>
22.q What is the formula of the salt that consists of Na<sup>+</sup> and SO<sub>4</sub><sup>2−</sup> ions?
22.a Na<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>
23.q What is the formula of the salt that consists of Ca<sup>2+</sup> and NO<sub>3</sub><sup>−</sup> ions?
23.a Ca(NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>
24.q What is the name of the salt with formula Na<sub>2</sub>S?
24.a Sodium sulfide
25.q How are the ions in a salt held together?
25.a By the attraction between positive and negative ions
26.q When does a salt conduct electric current?
26.a When it is dissolved or molten
27.q Why do salts have a high melting point?
27.a The attraction between the ions is strong
28.q How is a covalent bond formed?
28.a Atoms share one or more pairs of electrons
29.q Between which atoms does a covalent bond usually occur?
29.a Between non-metal atoms
30.q How many pairs of electrons do the two hydrogen atoms share in an H<sub>2</sub> molecule?
30.a 1
31.q Why do metals conduct electric current?
31.a They have freely moving electrons
32.q Which substance consists of molecules?
32.a Water
33.q Which substance conducts electric current in the solid state?
33.a Copper
34.q What is the van der Waals force?
34.a The weak attraction between molecules
